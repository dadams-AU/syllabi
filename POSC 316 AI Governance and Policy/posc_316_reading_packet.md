# How This Packet Works

This course assigns **one book** and an **article packet**. Every item other than the book is provided at no cost through *Canvas* and the Pollak Library.

Each week pairs **one scholarly reading with one accessible reading** — journalism, a plain-language explainer, or a primary regulatory document. That cap is deliberate: expect roughly **40–60 pages per week**. Weeks 6 through 8 lean on the book, so the article load drops.

There are **twelve reading weeks**, matching the twelve weekly reading responses. Weeks 9, 14, 15, and 16 assign no new reading so you can concentrate on the midterm, the audit fair, and your final report.

Some readings are marked *updated each offering*. AI policy moves quickly, so current investigative reporting, live case dossiers, and legislative trackers are refreshed each time the course runs and posted to *Canvas* at the start of the semester.

Short citations appear under each week. **Full citations for everything are listed at the end of this packet.**

# A Note on How These Readings Were Chosen

The list alternates disciplinary registers on purpose, because this course serves students in public administration and political science alongside students in computer science and cybersecurity.

Public administration and policy anchors come from *Public Administration Review*, the *American Review of Public Administration*, *Perspectives on Public Management and Governance*, and law reviews. Computing anchors come from FAccT, CHI, *Communications of the ACM*, *Science*, the NIST AI Risk Management Framework, and CISA secure-AI guidance.

**No reading assumes programming.** Technical readings are drawn from the framework and documentation genres — model cards, risk-management frameworks, security guidance — rather than mathematical or systems papers. They are assigned as documentation-literacy exercises that feed directly into the audit project. The one empirical technical paper, Obermeyer et al., is excerpted through its main results, paired with narrative journalism covering the same system, and supported by a reading guide.
